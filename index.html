Switch formats at any time. Single is locked to one conduit; Rack restores the normal multi-conduit workflow. Trim Mode (default ON) square-cuts each section to the shortest free end / second leg so all conduits line up; the Trim Off column is how much to cut off the other end of each longer stick. With Trim Mode off, full stick free ends remain and struts can land on those stubs.
Auto Space calculates each adjacent CTC from the two conduit outside diameters plus a 9/16-inch clear gap. Turn Auto Space off to enter CTC values manually.

Which side is conduit 1. Parallel/rolled mark stagger also follows offset direction so bends open the rack instead of closing into neighbors.
OAL is finished linear distance A to B after the offset (shortened by shrink). Stick developed = OAL + shrink. Not back of 90. Orange when ON.
Push Through (default): Bend 2 = Center + shrink, then shift every mark to the 45° rim notch. Δ = CLR × (45° − bend°) / 2 (radians) — Ideal rim notch is the COB of a 45° bend; mid-arc COB of θ is CLR×θ/2 from bend start. Requires conduit size for CLR and OD.
Different enables a separate height for the second opposing offset.
OAL is finished linear A→B after the saddle (shortened by total shrink). Stick developed = OAL + shrink. Not back of 90. Orange when ON.
Back of 90 is the finished outside of the 90 (layout station) — not OAL. OAL means finished A to B overall after shrink. Start of Bend anchors the sweep tangent.
Rack radii change concentrically so the entered CTC and conduit OD gaps remain constant through the sweep.
The 90 is divided into nine equal 10-degree shots. Marks are centered within nine equal developed-arc sections.
